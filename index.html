About Raja Thandai

Located at Gole Darwaza Chowk, opposite Santoshi Mata Mandir in Machchhi Bhavan, Raja Thandai is a family-run shop that has been serving Lucknow since 1936. For generations, it has been a much-loved stop in the old city, and a true landmark during Holi.

The secret is in the preparation. The thandai masala is hand-ground daily on a traditional stone grinder, then blended with milk, saffron, pistachios, cashew nuts, cardamom, fennel and the traditional five seed kernels for the rich, cooling flavour the shop is famous for.
